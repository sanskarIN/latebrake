# Community code of conduct

Latebrake is a public project where everyone should be able to collaborate respectfully.

## Expected conduct

Be welcoming to contributors of different experience levels and backgrounds. Give constructive code feedback, explain technical trade-offs, respect other people's time, and credit original work.

## Unacceptable conduct

Harassment, threats, bullying, discriminatory slurs, unwanted personal contact, publication of private information, and deliberate disruption of issue/PR discussions are not allowed.

## Moderation and reporting

Maintainers may edit, hide, or remove harmful content and restrict participation to protect the community. For conduct-related reports, contact **sanskarprogrammer@gmail.com** or **sanskarcodes@outlook.com** and provide URLs or a brief account of what happened. Reports should be handled as privately as practical.

Participants should follow the rules on the code host and the platform on which discussions take place.
