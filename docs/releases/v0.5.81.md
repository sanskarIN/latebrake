## Changes since v0.5.80

### Visual camera handling
- The local human race camera now gradually adjusts its lens by up to six
  degrees from genuine positive chassis forward speed, bounded by the
  selected preset and disabled for AI controllers, reverse and rear view.
- Camera adjustments reset on invalid physics, pause, restart, recovery
  and interrupted focus. They never alter vehicle forces or race results.

### Player-held prototype handbrake
- Hold **Left Shift** or **right gamepad stick click** for a separate
  prototype handbrake action. On the existing single-Chaos-body vehicle
  it gates motor throttle, reduces lateral resistance and applies
  bounded longitudinal braking. The original service BRAKE still
  takes precedence when both are held.
- A desktop HUD flag indicates held handbrake state. Pauses, menus,
  focus loss, restart, recovery and control release clear the input.
- This is **not** a new rear-wheel tire simulation, ABS or hydraulic model.

### Observed braking feedback
- The noncompact HUD can show a guarded **in-game** stopping-distance
  projection at the current rate of deceleration, with meters or feet
  following existing speed-display settings.
- It requires multiple consecutive, finite, physically plausible
  chassis speed samples while braking. Single commands, reverse,
  sideways slides, unrealistic speed jumps, collisions and interrupts
  cannot produce stale or unearned distances. It is **not** a reliable
  real-world stopping-distance instrument.
- This telemetry affects no credits, times, saved records or physics.
