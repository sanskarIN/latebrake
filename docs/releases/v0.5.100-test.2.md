## Source changes since v0.5.100-test.1

The protected `main` source now includes the previously developed, separately
reviewed portable racing modules merged in [PR #395](https://github.com/sanskarIN/latebrake/pull/395):

- **Race-engineering telemetry:** source-only lap and sector analytics,
  session timelines, vehicle health and reliability, fuel and pit strategy,
  driver comparisons, replay buffers, coaching and training reviews.
- **Original circuit research:** physical-road planning, track-limit source
  policies, a proposed world-scale centerline, surface geometry and
  deterministic curvature-aware pace estimates.
- **Vehicle-model research:** independent suspension, wheel-brake, contact,
  speed-limit and grip-budget algorithms that remain C++20 prototypes,
  not a newly activated Chaos Vehicles simulation.
- **AI traffic and racing policies:** candidate six-car starting-grid
  reasoning, rival safety forecasts and route/traffic checks.
- **New continuous-motion advisory audit:** a pure-C++ circuit pace policy
  cross-checks observed XY chassis motion, actor/session identity, road
  support, frame order and route progress. Discontinuities and impossible
  provenance reset evidence rather than granting race time or reward.
- **Compatibility hardening:** historic coaching, pit, and fuel subsystem
  type-name conflicts were resolved; stale tests for APIs not in current
  `main` were removed rather than changing gameplay behavior.

These modules include **47 additional standalone regression targets**,
with C++20 source tests exercised under strict compiler diagnostics.
The scope of this integration is portable C++ and tests; it has **not
been connected to new player, GameMode, or rival Unreal runtime behavior**.
