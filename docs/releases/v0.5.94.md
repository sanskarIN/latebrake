## Focused driving improvement since v0.5.93

### Physical reverse overspeed recovery

- Replace the real player/rival Chaos pawn's hard reverse-overspeed
  `SetPhysicsLinearVelocity` clamp with a **finite, signed, planar
  `Chassis->AddForce`** request. Preserve actual sideways skid and
  vertical gravity, instead of instantly replacing XY momentum after
  a collision.
- Retain the original **48.6 km/h Arcade** and **36 km/h Simulation
  Inspired** reverse-speed values as **soft targets**, not guaranteed
  instantaneous hard caps. Transient collision and road/physics forces
  can legitimately exceed them.
- Limit recovery to **850 cm/s² Arcade** or **650 cm/s² Simulation**
  *before* actual surface, tire, garage, damage and supported rear-axle
  reductions. Support loss or airborne travel never synthesizes a
  braking force.
- Block new reverse-motor thrust near the measured reverse-speed
  target without blocking ordinary forward drive, low-speed reversing,
  genuine opposite-pedal braking, race time or scoring. Do not count
  blocked reverse input as engine work that generates artificial heat.
- Give service/handbrake and opposite-pedal braking precedence over
  the governor. Budget the physical reverse-correction force against
  the **same-frame lateral traction** instead of stacking unrealistic
  braking and sideways force.
- Preserve the old `ArcadeReverseSpeedLimit.h` as a pure **historical
  numerical test reference**; the accepted Unreal drive Tick does not
  call its velocity-projection function.

### Regression and source binding improvements

- Add a strict standalone C++20 `ReverseSpeedGovernor` regression:
  100,000 deterministic heading/velocity/contact/grip/pedal samples;
  an additional **60,000 combined reverse-braking + lateral-grip**
  force compositions against the shared friction budget; 30/60/120 FPS
  integration and settling; zero-contact, invalid-input, reverse-drift,
  service-brake and gear-transition conditions.
- Synchronize the existing actual Unreal source-binding checks for
  reverse motor, gear power, yaw steering, planar speed management,
  human/rival telemetry order and the non-shipping handling log.
- Document [physical reverse-speed acceptance](
  https://github.com/sanskarIN/latebrake/blob/feat/chaos-four-corner-suspension/docs/REVERSE_OVERSPEED_FORCE_QA.md)
  separately from the legacy instantaneous reference.
