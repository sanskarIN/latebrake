## Changes since v0.5.83

### OFF-PAVEMENT TRACTION — measured circuit surface response

- The actual shared player/rival physical chassis reads its own
  `GetActorLocation()` XY coordinates after circuit readiness, valid
  race start, live Chaos body and finite-velocity checks, and classifies
  its position against the original four-strip
  `IsOnPavedSurface` track geometry. This is not a tire contact sensor,
  a material query, wheel friction or another fake map.
- **0.15 seconds continuously off pavement** confirms a lower-grip
  arcade response; **0.20 seconds continuously on pavement** restores
  baseline handling. Transient edge crossings cannot flicker force
  values every tick.
- Confirmed off-pavement driving applies **78%** of the existing
  forward motor, already-bounded human NITRO BOOST force and qualified
  SLIPSTREAM force, and **72%** of existing lateral damping. The
  original ARCADE TIRE temperature/wear multiplier remains applied;
  frame-stable resistance safeguards and official speed cap remain.
  On road, motor and lateral scales are exactly 100%.
- Finite-frame sampling and physically plausible world coordinates
  are mandatory. Invalid physics, pause/menu/focus interruption,
  restart, recovery and teleport must not reuse stale surface state or
  grant a fabricated motor boost.
- Expanded desktop HUD can show confirmed
  `OFF-PAVEMENT TRACTION` and the active motor/grip multipliers while
  protecting compact/mobile controls and authoritative lap timing.
  Existing off-track directional guidance remains independent.
- No steering commands, race gate checks, lap timing, AI routing,
  career credits, rewards, record saves or official classification
  are changed by the surface policy.
- [Surface traction source and real driving QA](https://github.com/sanskarIN/latebrake/blob/v0.5.84/docs/ARCADE_SURFACE_TRACTION_QA.md).

### Previously qualified racing features preserved

- **RIVAL GRIP** source-level AI corner-entry pacing continues to
  use sampled tire grip and chassis slip; player-aware traffic
  braking and the separate brake holds retain their precedence.
- Existing run-local ARCADE TIRE, recharge-limited human NITRO BOOST,
  geometry-verified SLIPSTREAM and capped combined force bonus remain.
- The shared pavement multiplier applies to all three motor paths,
  so human nitro or drafting cannot bypass reduced off-road force.

## Independent qualification

The publisher must compile and run **all** standalone C++20 tests on
Windows, Ubuntu and macOS at one qualified immutable main commit.
It additionally runs nonempty, fail-closed selected tests for
**ArcadeSurfaceTraction**, RivalGripPace, ArcadeTireCondition,
ArcadeNitroBoost, SlipstreamDraftBoost and ArcadeBoostArbitration.
The surface suite includes authored pavement boundaries, boundary
chatter, invalid samples and 250,000 deterministic numerical frames.

Python Unreal-*source* contracts require actual shared pawn integration
and force ordering, while independent PowerShell negative packaging
fixtures and MkDocs site verification are also mandatory. None of
these tools execute the actual licensed Unreal runtime.

The release action verifies that v0.5.83 is already a published,
asset-free source pre-release, refuses tag drift, rejects a main commit
change during qualification, and creates only the immutable source tag
and pre-release with **zero executable binaries**.
