## Real source changes since published v0.5.89

### PHYSICAL CHASSIS DRIVING — PRs #281–#285

- **ProgressiveRoadLoad** replaces excessive constant coast damping with
  bounded rolling/aerodynamic road load. Player and physical rival use
  the same policy; off-pavement still adds resistance.
- **SpeedAwareYawTorque** makes steering depend on actual lateral
  chassis motion and speed instead of arbitrary zero-speed turning.
- **AutomaticTransmission** adds a four-forward-gear/reverse automatic
  drive state, shift torque reductions and engine-speed telemetry.
- **GripLimitedBraking** bounds forward/reverse service braking against
  real measured planar speed, road and tire grip, damage and thermal fade.
- **LateralGripBudget** caps lateral force and shares an approximate
  combined traction budget with actual braking. This is a single Chaos
  rigid-body approximation, **not** a validated wheel/tire contact model.

### RIVAL CORNER AND TRAFFIC DRIVING — PRs #286–#288

- **RivalCornerBrakeEnvelope** forecasts earlier corner braking from
  live previous-tick grip/thermal measurements, current measured speed
  and remaining inbound gate distance.
- **RivalStraightPassAssist** supplies genuine bounded steering
  for a single AI car passing a slower player only on a qualified
  original paved straight. This is not multiplayer or full opponent AI.
- **RivalPassTrafficResolution** fixes an unsafe conflict: the
  independent real-velocity traffic emergency brake now cancels
  overtaking and restores original route steering instead of braking
  with an active sideways passing steer. Passing starts conservatively
  only on the two longer original 5600cm straights, with adequate
  corner distance and measured closing-time margin.
- Player-authoritative checkpoints, rewards, singleplayer save
  authority, AI recovery and the independent traffic safety brake
  remain unchanged. The steering change is not proof of physical
  collision-free passes on a cooked Unreal map.

### Other retained development since v0.5.89
