## Changes since v0.5.38

- **FOCUS-LOSS PHYSICS:** If an operating system deactivates the app but the Unreal world refuses to pause, the car no longer intentionally retains its drive commands and previous rigid-body momentum while the competitive race clock is suspended. The pawn neutralizes raw and smoothed driving inputs and zeroes chassis linear/angular velocities immediately after a new guarded focus loss and during surviving pawn ticks.
- **Fail-closed mobile touch:** While the focus-loss latch is active, touch input cannot newly accelerate, steer, brake, recover, restart or select career actions. A fresh PAUSE press alone is allowed to invoke the existing explicit player-resume path. A held finger, drag, or duplicate press cannot silently resume competition.
- **Tests and source contracts:** New portable C++20 `FocusSuspendedTouchPolicy` matrix and CMake registration, plus Python `FocusSuspendedDriverBindingTests` asserting that actual Unreal pawn source gates physics and touch dispatch in the right order.
- **QA documentation:** Added a real-world test plan covering nonzero vehicle momentum, pause failure, foregrounding, resume controls, duplicate OS callbacks and physical Android input.
