## New source since v0.5.78

### Faster lap as a visible, run-local reference

- The real possessed, physics-ready car now feeds a bounded C++20
  best-lap pose recorder from the *same qualified post-GO race clock*
  already used for authentic circuit checkpoints.
- Only a genuinely completed ordered-checkpoint lap with verified
  active clock advancement, finite time and no recovery penalty can
  replace the in-memory fastest trace. Invalid physics, player swaps,
  pauses, focus loss, restarts, teleports, sample hitches, malformed
  data or impossible motion never fabricate a best lap.
- After a valid lap, a **small non-colliding floating reference beacon**
  follows the interpolated best-run driving path on later laps. It is
  **not** a transparent full-car ghost, AI racer, official leaderboard
  result, persistent replay or saved personal-best lap.
- The source policy stores at most 4,096 keyframes, samples at about
  10 Hz, interpolates shortest-angle yaw and rejects wrong/infinite
  replay times. HUD and Results label this as observational, RUN ONLY.
- Menu opening, deliberate pause, OS focus interruption, paid
  recovery, results finish and restart hide the marker immediately
  even when paused worlds do not Tick. No collision, navigation,
  rigid-body force or scoring authority comes from this marker.

### Vehicle braking and reversing are now visible

- Two small prototype red rear point lights follow the actual
  effective brake state rather than a guessed wheel spin.
- A white rear reverse lamp responds only to measured **negative**
  longitudinal chassis velocity, not a held reverse throttle.
- Lighting rejects invalid physics, pauses and absent car; it is
  cosmetic only and never alters race clock, AI steering, physics,
  controller input, driver rankings, scores or saved garage.
- Lights use short attenuation, no dynamic shadows or extra GI,
  and are subject to real Win64/Android renderer support and
  performance validation. They are not emissive lens materials.
