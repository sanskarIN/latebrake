## Changes since v0.5.44

- **VEHICLE DYNAMICS READINESS:** A new production C++20 header and implementation, `VehicleDynamicsReadiness.h` and `VehicleDynamicsReadiness.cpp`, validate positive finite physics mass and finite X/Y/Z chassis angular velocity. Previously only physics mode, collision, position and *linear* speed were checked before a vehicle was treated as race-ready.
- **Authoritative race eligibility:** The real Unreal player pawn includes the new dynamics policy in `IsRacePhysicsReady()`, which the GameMode already checks for active race clock, paid recovery and ordered checkpoint scoring. An unhealthy chassis is no longer eligible for those activities.
- **Vehicle physics protection:** The pawn checks complete readiness in its actual Unreal Tick before smoothing inputs or adding motor force and steering torque. Blocked bodies have held controls and existing linear/angular motion neutralized.
- **Rival AI safety:** The autonomous car verifies *its own* chassis health before updating route/lap approximation instead of relying only on the player's valid body.
- **Reliable HUD telemetry:** Nonfinite raw linear velocity or computed speed produces a finite 0 km/h reading instead of propagating NaN/Inf to the overlay.
- **Source regressions and QA:** Strict portable `VehicleDynamicsReadinessTests.cpp` compiles the production policy `.cpp`, links actual `RaceProgress.cpp` and covers zero/negative/nonfinite mass, all three angular axes, frozen timing and healthy resumption. New Unreal-source Python integration contracts check live call sites and ordering. See `docs/VEHICLE_DYNAMICS_QA.md` for genuine build/device acceptance.
