## Proposed changes, contingent on PR #397 being merged

- New portable C++20 `SixCarRouteContinuityAudit` that validates an entire
  six-actor sample together, rather than accepting one missing car or
  treating an array of simulated IDs as a real Unreal six-car field.
- Independent actor tokens and grid slots, race-session identity,
  consecutive frame ordinals, physical road-support observations,
  world XY positions and *measured* velocities.
- A proposed original world-scale circuit centreline projection, compared
  independently with reported lap station and unwrapped completed-lap
  counters. This is an advisory model, NOT official checkpoint authority.
- Tight per-frame world-position and reported route-motion consistency
  checks. Use **signed circuit-tangent velocity**, not nonnegative
  speedometer magnitude, so backward and sideways motion cannot invent
  forward progress or a credited overtake.
- Per-pair route-order hysteresis; no double-counted advisory passes due
  to small alignment noise. New leader must demonstrate forward route
  motion to qualify a source-only pass observation.
- Deterministic source-only C++20 regressions at 30/60/120 FPS for
  reordered six-car input, missing/duplicate IDs, forged lap records,
  dropped frames, race-session change, pause/recovery, lap seam, frozen
  positions, wrong-way travel, sideways slips and legitimate forward drift.

**NOT included:** the still-open native-physics and gameplay changes
from PR #377 (3D drafting), #391 (reverse powertrain behavior), or
#394 (grip-limited braking); source tests do not ship those changes.
This proposed source-only module does not create a live six-car race.
