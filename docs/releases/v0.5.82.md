## Changes since v0.5.81

### ARCADE TIRE condition — shared race-local grip behavior
- A finite, bounded source policy estimates tire condition from measured
  planar/lateral rigid-body chassis speed and effective braking demand.
- Sustained lateral sliding and hard braking increase arcade wear/heat
  and reduce only the existing frame-stable chassis lateral damping,
  with a minimum 68% multiplier.
- Both the human driver and the physical rival participate; an accepted
  new race resets conditions, but pause, interruption and ordinary
  recovery do not silently repair worn tires.
- This is NOT a real tire compound, wheel collision or suspension model.
  [Tire condition QA](https://github.com/sanskarIN/latebrake/blob/v0.5.82/docs/ARCADE_TIRE_CONDITION_QA.md).

### NITRO BOOST — held human acceleration ability
- Press and hold **B** (keyboard) or **left gamepad thumbstick click**
  during eligible human forward racing. Bounded energy, heat, regeneration
  and cooldown make the power-up finite and require a fresh press after
  thermal cutoff or depletion.
- The prototype applies at most 38% extra baseline forward motor force
  and a smoothed speed-cap target increase of at most 12%.
- Reject nonfinite frame/velocity, braking, reverse, bad physics, missing
  controller, menu, pause, focus loss, recovered/stale input, and finished
  racing. AI does not receive human nitro commands. This is not real
  nitrous chemistry or engine thermodynamics.
- [Nitro input, source and hardware QA](https://github.com/sanskarIN/latebrake/blob/v0.5.82/docs/NITRO_BOOST_QA.md).

### SLIPSTREAM — measured single-rival racecraft
- A local human following the genuine valid moving rival can earn a
  position-dependent arcade advantage of at most **+6% motor force**
  only after at least 0.18 seconds of continuous qualification.
- Real position, planar headings, signed speeds, line alignment and
  separation must satisfy bounded and physically plausible thresholds.
  Oncoming, behind, too distant, off-lane, vertically separated,
  stationary, invalid, finished and missing rivals cannot yield a bonus.
- Drafting never controls AI steering/braking, official lap times,
  checkpoints, credit payouts, saved garage profiles or race records.
  It is not an aerodynamics simulation or multiplayer position tracker.
- [Slipstream source and gameplay QA](https://github.com/sanskarIN/latebrake/blob/v0.5.82/docs/SLIPSTREAM_DRAFT_QA.md).

## Qualification policy

Before this release can publish, the publisher independently runs the
**entire** portable C++20 suite on Windows, macOS and Ubuntu, plus
focused tests for ArcadeTireCondition, ArcadeNitroBoost,
SlipstreamDraftBoost and the existing race stability/input guards.
It also runs Python Unreal-source contracts (without the Unreal runtime),
synthetic Windows PowerShell package-provenance checks and MkDocs
documentation verification at one immutable main SHA.

Tag publication refuses unexpected main advancement, a changed or
binary-attached v0.5.81 source pre-release, or a conflicting v0.5.82 tag.
There is no packaging job and no downloadable compiled game attachment.
