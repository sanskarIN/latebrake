## Improvements over source-only v0.5.19

- **Strict completed-lap sequence:** The bounded `RecentTimedLaps` buffer rejects equal or decreasing completed-lap numbers, so replayed/duplicated events cannot silently replace the authoritative last-lap data.
- **Preserved ring-buffer semantics:** The most recent eight legitimate timed laps survive rejections correctly even after the ring wraps. Skipped but increasing IDs remain valid; reset clears the previous attempt's history.
- **Regression tests:** The original portable `RecentTimedLapsTests.cpp` suite now checks duplicates, backdated writes, wrapping, next valid insertion, and clean race reset. Portable `RaceProgress` and race-pace projections remain independently tested.
- **No invented records:** The source validation neither invents competitive finish times nor awards player credits based on invalid lap history.
