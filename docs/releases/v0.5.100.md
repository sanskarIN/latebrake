## What is included in this candidate

- **PR #377** tightens the optional human-only slipstream boost against
  impossible real-player-versus-rival **three-dimensional relative
  relocation**, including measured Chaos vertical velocities for both cars.
  Missing, infinite, implausibly large or discontinuous measurements cannot
  silently reuse an earlier earned drafting interval. An independent
  **75 cm unexplained vertical residual** limit prevents a smaller in-lane
  Z relocation from hiding beneath the wider 2 m world-space allowance.
- The same source-only C++20 policy now normalizes both nearly-unit planar
  forward vectors before computing true relative heading alignment,
  longitudinal gap, lateral lane distance and predicted planar motion. A
  slightly oversized direction vector must not make a skewed rival appear aligned,
  or falsely reject an otherwise legal near-edge drafting gap.
- The physically verified unobstructed path, live rival, at least three
  real road contacts for **both** cars, lateral-slip eligibility, unchanged
  minimum speeds and 0.18-second uninterrupted confirmation remain
  mandatory. Invalid geometry or lost physics clears accumulated credit.
- The optional arcade drafting effect is limited to **+6% additional motor
  force**, sharing a maximum **40% combined extra motor-force budget** with
  genuine earned nitro. The feature does not govern braking, wheels,
  camera, checkpoint identity, official lap times, classification,
  progression, saves, credits or rewards.
- The source includes unit regressions for vertical motion, NaN/Infinity
  and missing physical measurements, interrupted qualification,
  rotated/near-unit heading spoofing, bounded lateral/forward offsets
  and physical lane continuity. Python tests assert the actual Unreal
  C++ callsite bindings; they do not execute Unreal.
