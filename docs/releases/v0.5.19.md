## Changes following the source-only v0.5.18 preview

- **Finite autonomous recovery budget:** `RivalRouteProgress` now refuses requests for additional AI teleports after its finite session recovery allowance is exhausted, instead of silently continuing to request teleports that would never be represented in the counter.
- **Counted recovery invariant:** Every accepted autonomous recovery request corresponds to an increment of the recorded recovery count. A completed or reset event cannot import stale counts from an earlier attempt.
- **Boundary stress regression:** Original `RivalRouteTests.cpp` exhausts the entire recovery budget, verifies that the next teleport is refused, tests the denial after movement, and ensures a new race attempt has a fresh budget.
- **Separate player timing:** These route-recovery counts do not constitute competitive lap results, standings or a verified AI race finish.
