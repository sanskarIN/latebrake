## New features since v0.5.84

### BRAKE HEAT — bounded, race-local service-brake fade

- The shared player and AI rival pawn now samples actual finite chassis
  forward speed and ordinary service-brake demand through the production
  C++20 `ArcadeBrakeHeat` policy.
- Sustained high-speed braking increases synthetic heat from its ambient
  floor. Heat beyond 62% can progressively reduce existing service-brake
  resistance, but never below **74%** of its unfaded baseline.
- Releasing the service brake cools the measured history. Ordinary
  handbrake-only slides cannot raise service-brake heat, and the existing
  parking-brake resistance floor remains when both brakes are pressed.
- Pause, focus loss, invalid frames, restart and teleport fail closed,
  while normal race recovery cannot invent a free thermal reset.
- Expanded desktop HUD reports `BRAKE HEAT` and effective service-brake
  percentage only when a qualified high-heat measurement exists.
  Small Android touch HUD rows are not expanded.
- This is an arcade approximation. It is **not** physical rotor
  temperature, real ABS, hydraulic pressure, tire compound testing,
  or installed vehicle gameplay.
- [Brake-heat implementation and real-engine QA](https://github.com/sanskarIN/latebrake/blob/main/docs/ARCADE_BRAKE_HEAT_QA.md).

### IMPACT DAMAGE — actual Chaos chassis-hit source wiring

- The shared player/rival root body registers Unreal's physical
  `OnComponentHit` callback and receives collision impulse, impact
  normal, body mass and observed current/recent qualified planar speed.
- Only genuinely eligible post-GO driving, significant side-on impacts
  and plausible measured values may add damage. Ground contact,
  low-speed nudges, invalid/nonfinite physics, pre-race overlap and
  interrupted/paused callbacks are rejected.
- A **0.40-second contact cooldown** suppresses repeated hit callbacks
  from one contact event. Each accepted hard impact adds between
  **3% and 13%** damage, capped at **60% per race**.
- New damage scales the existing shared motor force, human nitro and
  approved slipstream *together* to at least **79%** of baseline,
  and lateral grip to at least **85.6%**. It cannot increase steering
  or motor power, add race penalties or change authoritative scoring.
- Restart resets the player and rival damage; normal pause, recovery,
  teleport or focus suspension only suspends hit eligibility, so
  collisions cannot be fabricated across a physics interruption.
- Expanded desktop HUD can show `IMPACT DAMAGE` alongside actual motor
  and grip multipliers. Compact mobile controls remain unchanged.
- These are source-level engine bindings, not evidence that actual
  Unreal 5.8/Chaos component-hit events work in an installed game.
- [Collision impact source and real-engine QA](https://github.com/sanskarIN/latebrake/blob/main/docs/ARCADE_COLLISION_DAMAGE_QA.md).

### Previously published racing systems preserved

- `OFF-PAVEMENT TRACTION` v0.5.84 remains bound to the real physical
  track position and applies the existing 78% motor and 72% grip scales
  after road-edge hysteresis. Damage is composed with this existing
  multiplier, not substituted for it.
- `ARCADE TIRE` condition, `RIVAL GRIP` pacing, `NITRO BOOST`,
  `SLIPSTREAM`, shared `ArcadeBoostArbitration` and the portable
  `FrameStableDamping` safeguard retain their authority boundaries.
- Checkpoint order, three-lap timing, earned credits, race results,
  career progression, saved records and profile schema are unchanged.

## Qualification requirements for this source release

- Compile and execute the complete standalone C++20 test suite on
  **Windows, Ubuntu and macOS** at one exact, immutable source SHA.
- Explicitly require nonempty passing `ArcadeBrakeHeat` and
  `ArcadeCollisionDamage` CTests as well as legacy surface traction,
  tire, nitro, slipstream, rival grip and motor arbitration policies.
- The two feature suites cover heat/cooling/thermal bounds,
  real-impact threshold/cooldown/cap, invalid NaN/Inf inputs, resets,
  suspended state and **250,000 deterministic numerical frames each**.
- Run every Python Unreal-*source* integration contract, PowerShell
  candidate-manifest and fake-archive rejection fixture, and render and
  verify the MkDocs documentation site.
- Fail publication if `main` advances between the tested SHA and
  publication, or if an existing tag points to a different SHA.
  Publish a pre-release with **zero executable binaries**, not a stable
  release. No job claims to build Unreal Engine.

## Still required for a playable game

1. Compile licensed Unreal Engine **5.8/UHT** Editor and Game targets.
2. Create/cook the real, owned circuit and verify its Chaos collision
   contact geometry; prove actual player and rival collision callbacks.
3. Test and tune collision impulses, dynamic brake heat, tire
   handling, road-edge traction, nitro/slipstream blending and AI
   over genuine installed Windows gameplay at different frame rates.
4. Qualify genuine Win64 and Android packages with installation logs,
   engine fingerprint, map/source hashes and actual touch/gamepad QA.
5. Keep [playable release gate #148](https://github.com/sanskarIN/latebrake/issues/148)
   and [Android device gate #13](https://github.com/sanskarIN/latebrake/issues/13)
   open pending that evidence.
