## New source since v0.5.79

### Rival and lap information
- **Best-lap ghost separation estimate**: bounded observational distance feedback to the run-local best-lap reference beacon. No official scoring, saved ghost, or multiplayer opponent is invented.
- **Guarded ghost display input**: source policies for a desktop/controller toggle and Android touch pad keep visibility separate from race physics and results.
- **Predictive following brake**: AI uses signed longitudinal closing speed, finite time-to-contact and bounded brake hysteresis for safer in-lane following. It must not mistake sideways drift for an impending collision.

### Physics, controls, and camera
- **Frame-stable damping**: bounded grip/braking/reverse resistance prevents oversized forces from transient frame hitches; real Chaos handling must still be tested in Unreal.
- **Android neutral steering band**: reduced center jitter and immediate residual steering cancellation, preserving independent pedals.
- **Chassis slip-angle readout**: live signed direction-of-travel estimate and read-only session peaks in the expanded desktop HUD/Results; this is not a tire slip or stability-control model.
- **Held look-back camera**: hold **V** or the gamepad **left shoulder** for a rear view, release to restore the selected preset; pause, restart, recovery and focus loss clear the transient view.
