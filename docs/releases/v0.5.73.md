## New source work beyond v0.5.72

### Engine-independent arcade drift combos
- A bounded, in-memory `DriftComboMeter` consumes the same actual
  sampled speed and sideways chassis motion as the driving telemetry,
  after real player drive readiness, physics and finite-velocity checks.
- It requires continuous qualifying virtual lateral motion before any
  score activates; accepts only brief nonqualifying interruptions,
  rejects nonfinite, unrealistically large or hitch-duration frames,
  and clamps points and sample time to finite limits.
- World pause, app focus loss, physics invalidity, recoveries and
  race finish suspend and bank one combo without double counting.
  Genuine race restart resets the entire meter and old session score.
- A spacious live racing HUD shows provisional combo time and points.
  The existing paged finished Results menu reports total arcade
  points, combo count, best combo and qualified slip sample seconds
  *only for valid recordable finishes*.
- UI explicitly labels the detail **ARCADE DRIFT SCORE (NO CREDITS)**.
  Points cannot change career, race clock, vehicle handling, saved
  best times, credits, records, rewards, purchases or leaderboard.
  It is a virtual game style feature, not real-world driving guidance.
