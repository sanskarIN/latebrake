## New source work since v0.5.21

- **Cancel a malformed held finger:** If a finger already owns throttle, brake or steering, a duplicate press with NaN or infinite coordinates now neutralizes that finger's control instead of leaving the last held driving value latched.
- **Preserve other controls:** A different valid held finger is not interrupted by the malformed event. A malformed unowned finger cannot steal or clear unrelated controls, and a valid duplicate press leaves its held input unchanged.
- **Regression coverage:** Expanded engine-independent `TouchDriveStateTests.cpp` and `TouchFingerRangeTests.cpp` exercise malformed held gas and steering input, the maximum representable finger index, independent controls, and late movement after owner cancellation.
- Existing racing checkpoints, touch viewport fail-safe, measured lap forecast, AI safety, recovery counters and validated lap history remain in the project source.
