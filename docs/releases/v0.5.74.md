## v0.5.74 source improvements over v0.5.73

- A pure C++20 human controller-input deadzone policy filters signed
  steering input through a **12%** symmetric center zone, and
  throttle/reverse triggers through a **5.5%** symmetric zone.
  The rest of each analog axis is smoothly remapped back onto its
  full range; `-1` and `+1` keyboard/analog extremes remain full
  authority, while centered controller jitter outputs exactly zero.
- The policy is installed in the actual UE pawn's `SteerInput` and
  `ThrottleInput` player callbacks, not the autonomous rival
  `SetAutonomousControls` route or separate Android touch pads.
- Nonfinite/raw-out-of-range inputs and invalid thresholds are
  rejected or bounded, without affecting ordinary brake commands,
  existing input-focus interruption, vehicle handling, race clock,
  rewards, save schema, online accounts or leaderboards.
- Strict portable C++20 tests cover sign symmetry, deadzone threshold,
  continuous scaled travel, full keyboard/trigger endpoints, dense
  monotonic sweep, NaN/Infinity and malformed configuration.
  Python tests check the real Unreal callback wiring and actual
  keyboard/gamepad `DefaultInput.ini` mappings.

provenance rejection fixtures, and rendered documentation integrity.

unqualified previous **v0.5.73** source-only pre-release.
