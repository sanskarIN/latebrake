## Implemented physics source

- Allocate the existing bounded service-brake request over actual positive
  four-corner spring loads and apply it at the same WorldStatic ray's genuine
  road ImpactPoint with Chaos `AddForceAtLocation`, instead of the chassis
  centre. This allows real body pitching and asymmetric contact moments.
- Measure genuine velocity at each road point. Suppress a brake share that
  would add local mechanical energy during a spin; fade near zero tire-point
  motion and never redistribute rejected shares to other corners.
- Limit handbrake-only force to loaded rear contacts. An airborne rear axle
  cannot acquire a front-brake fallback. Service plus handbrake still uses
  one admitted request.
- Construct a unit **road-tangent** brake direction from the actual hit normal
  and planar chassis heading. Uphill/downhill braking acquires the proper
  vertical component without applying force through the road normal or
  inventing sideways braking on banked roads.
- Spend the full 3D brake magnitude in the same-frame lateral/yaw friction
  budget. Keep the unchanged drive-log schema honest: `service_force`
  reports the actual aggregate planar-forward projection, not 3D magnitude.
- Preserve bounded spring/anti-roll support, front/rear load authority,
  physical reverse-speed recovery, nitro delivery/thermal receipts and the
  current main rear-lamp alignment and strict recent-lap statistics fixes.
