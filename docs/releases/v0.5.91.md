## New source since v0.5.90

### Actual four-corner rigid-body suspension research

- Four independent, measured **WorldStatic** ground traces at shared
  front-left, front-right, rear-left and rear-right wheel anchors.
- Each accepted corner samples actual **Chaos point velocity**, validated
  road-normal slope and spring compression, then submits bounded vertical
  `AddForceAtLocation` forces to the physical chassis.
- Unlocked real chassis pitch and roll, limited axle-local anti-roll
  load transfer, mass/gravity-aware spring force bounds and measured
  front-steering/rear-drive axle support. Partially unloaded or airborne
  wheels never receive an invented rear motor or front steering authority.
- Visual-only spindle travel uses the same canonical corner geometry,
  without decorative wheel movement feeding back into physics probes.
- Controlled reacquisition of rear motor and front steering grip after
  genuine recontact, while immediate load losses cut authorization.
- Non-shipping `LATEBRAKE_SUSPENSION v=1` diagnostic logs: first valid
  frame, rate-limited changes in measured supporting-wheel load,
  grid-to-GO transition and a low-frequency heartbeat. Brief events are
  latched until the next allowed record, but the log remains lossy and
  **is not physical validation**.

### Handling and source safety

- Near-vertical body attitudes retain real spring/gravity momentum
  without applying undefined planar motor or steering directions.
- Finite game-frame stalls above the supported 100 ms spring interval
  suppress invalid drive calculations without hard-zeroing valid Chaos
  linear/angular momentum.
- Six optional surface/drag/braking/grip/yaw/gearbox rejection paths
  revoke driving input and stale traction without freezing valid
  previously submitted chassis spring forces.
- Strict source bounds, isolated heave/pitch/roll test models,
  120k+ stress frames and source-binding tests guard against
  nonfinite/invalid contact or fabricated wheel support.

**Engineering scope:** these are four spring rays and force positions on
an Unreal rigid-body *prototype*, **not** independent Chaos Vehicles
wheel/tire contact dynamics, professional vehicle art, authored track
assets, or a verified 3D racing experience.
