## Source improvements since v0.5.31

- **Current-lap best-sector potential:** New `CurrentLapPotential.h` computes a purely informational current-lap estimate from actual measured positive sector records in the *same racing session*. It accounts for time already spent and never rewinds a slow current sector.
- **Expanded in-game HUD source:** The non-compact Unreal race HUD adds `LAP POTENTIAL (BEST SPLITS)` and signed `VS BEST`, comparing the estimate with a previously achieved lap. A faster theoretical estimate is **not** an awarded lap record.
- **Safer estimation:** No estimate appears until at least one timed lap, and incomplete sector references, zero-length splits, finished/restarted races and unrealistic clocks fail closed.
- **Regression tests:** `CurrentLapPotentialTests.cpp` compiles and links the real `RaceProgress.cpp` and covers four-gate and one-gate races, best-split updates, slow sectors, malformed references, final finish and reset.
- **Source and documentation consistency:** Updated engineering status, roadmap, release-history wording and documentation site.
