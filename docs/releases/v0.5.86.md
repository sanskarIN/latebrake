## Changes since the published v0.5.85 source preview

### ENGINE HEAT — shared bounded motor performance

- The physical player and AI rival rigid-body pawn now update a race-local,
  deterministic arcade heat fraction using qualified chassis forward speed,
  effective propulsion demand, and independently authorized human nitro.
  This is a gameplay abstraction, **not** engine oil/coolant temperature.
- Sustained propulsion above the synthetic **67% heat threshold** reduces
  base acceleration, nitro and slipstream power by the **same** multiplier,
  down to no less than **82%** of their prior force. Steering, ordinary
  service braking, opposite-throttle damping and final speed limits remain
  separate and unchanged.
- Coasting permits cooling. Invalid physics, frame hitches, focus loss,
  paused input and recovery never apply a stale penalty or silently create
  elapsed heat time; new racing sessions reset heat to ambient.
- The expanded desktop HUD offers conditional `ENGINE HEAT` and
  available motor readouts. Compact touch layouts are not enlarged.
- [Engine heat implementation and hardware QA](https://github.com/sanskarIN/latebrake/blob/main/docs/ARCADE_ENGINE_HEAT_QA.md).

### RIVAL HEAT PACE — observed real-rival cooldown

- The physical rival reads its previous **qualified** engine-heat sample
  after existing route, grip, surface and impact-aware decisions.
- At least 1,200 cm/s, heat above **70%** can gradually reduce the
  requested throttle, with a conservative **0.30 lower hot cap**. This
  source policy never invents rival rank, assists steering, applies new
  brakes, grants checkpoints or boosts motor force.
- Low-speed recovery, ordinary corner/traffic braking and verified
  route arrival remain authoritative; invalid physical samples fail
  closed. The base pawn retains its separate bounded engine-power model.
- [Rival heat pacing and installed-game QA](https://github.com/sanskarIN/latebrake/blob/main/docs/RIVAL_ENGINE_HEAT_PACE_QA.md).

### VERIFIED GATE GAP — honest discrete race feedback

- The experimental physical rival and player can be compared by
  **independently accepted forward directional checkpoint counts** on
  the same circuit event. The expanded desktop HUD labels the difference
  `VERIFIED GATE GAP` for clarity; counts are **NOT race place,
  official classification, timing difference or physical road lead**.
- Invalid lap/gate values, unavailable or unqualified chassis, pause,
  incorrect event, interrupted race and mismatch in circuit geometry
  suppress the comparison; it cannot change awards, save data or
  checkpoint authority.
- [Rival gate-gap verification](https://github.com/sanskarIN/latebrake/blob/main/docs/RIVAL_VERIFIED_GATE_GAP_QA.md).

### Other source racing improvements since v0.5.85

- Autonomous rivals must cross the real forward 3D checkpoint gate;
  a generic 440cm proximity arrival cannot credit a lap. The pilot
  aims through the gate instead of braking at the old radius.
- Additional source-level AI corner, surface, impact, yaw-rate and
  low-speed recovery guards; speed-aware motor taper, spin-aware human
  throttle reduction and advisory driving statistics.
- The legacy `BRAKE HEAT`, `IMPACT DAMAGE`,
  `OFF-PAVEMENT TRACTION`, `ARCADE TIRE`, `NITRO BOOST`,
  `SLIPSTREAM` and `RIVAL GRIP` systems remain bounded.
  No official player time, money, race-result, saved-profile,
  career reward or lap-clock format change is claimed.
