# Starter circuit specification — v0.4.0

## One canonical course

The active prototype remains **a single rectangular four-sector loop**. This update does not invent a second layout or claim a new track selection UI.

World distances are Unreal centimetres. The drivable road centerlines are at `Y = ±1800` on the east-west straights and `X = ±2800` on the north-south straights. Road half-width is `800 cm`. In order, checkpoints are east/south, east/north, west/north and west/south. Direction checks expect east, north, west and south travel respectively.

`Source/Latebrake/Public/Track/CircuitLayout.h` is the source of truth for those four gates, player/rival start positions and their recovery orientations. Both the Unreal game mode and the rival actor consume these common values. Player laps and recovery penalties have not been rebalanced.

## Visual course furnishings

`TrackDecorations.h` plans 28 white center dashes and 56 non-colliding decorative kerbs/curbs, alternating red and white, all on the existing asphalt. The runtime adds two primitive grandstands outside the drivable road and a visible overhead start/finish banner using only Unreal built-in cubes. Surface finish, materials, lighting, staging and camera angles remain engine-unverified.

The unreleased v0.5.1 source now uses a single scenery-holder actor with **seven** `UInstancedStaticMeshComponent` batches rather than 93 individual cosmetic actors. Batches cover 28 lane stripes, 28 red curbs, 28 white curbs, 2 grandstand bases, 4 light tiers, 2 dark tiers and 1 start/finish banner. All original scene positions, heights, scales and style assignments are generated by the portable deterministic `DecorationBatchPlan.h`. The **nine physical** ground/road/barrier blocks remain distinct, with unchanged physics and collision. Visual-only instances explicitly have collision, overlap events, navigation influence and cast shadows disabled.

The change reduces planned cosmetic actor/component overhead but **does not establish a particular FPS improvement or draw-call count** without a UE 5.8 Editor runtime profiling session. Stock Engine cube materials may not render the requested per-batch color parameter on every renderer; validate color contrast and readability on the actual target hardware. If required, author original project-owned, platform-cookable materials after the engine project builds.

The live GameMode emits `LATEBRAKE_SCENERY_READY groups=7 instances=93 collision=off` only when creation succeeds, otherwise `LATEBRAKE_SCENERY_INCOMPLETE`. This log reports component/instance construction, not correct rendering, cooking or playability.

## Course overview map

The pure `CircuitMap.h` helper normalizes finite world locations to a north-up 0–1 coordinate space with clamping for out-of-track positions. In wide desktop HUD layouts, the racing HUD renders a small track loop, player's current position and the next required checkpoint. On compact and mobile viewports it is intentionally omitted to avoid clutter.

## Validation requirements

Portable Windows/Linux/macOS CMake suites check this geometry and math, including fixed batch membership and unchanged legacy piece transforms. **They cannot establish** that a UE 5.8 game builds, materials/colors render correctly, that curbs never obstruct driving, or that the minimap avoids all HUD overlap. Real Unreal Editor tests and Win64/Android playtesting are required before a playable/stable release.
