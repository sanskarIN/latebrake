# Player sector motion review

This run-local feature works with the original four-gate circuit in
quick race, Time Attack, career and the opt-in experimental fleet.
GameMode observes the actual local chassis only after active clock
advance. Accepted ordered checkpoints close sector rows using the real
official split and independently charged fees. It does not change race
authority, physics, career credit, saved records or checkpoint tests.

Each interval requires adjacent engine frames, matching actor generation,
session and teleport revision, consistent active time and plausible body
displacement. No distance or duration is invented across a missing frame,
pause, recovery or gate boundary. Conditional durations require both
observed endpoints. Road footprint is projected decorative wheel geometry,
not native tyre contact. Lateral slip is a chassis-motion measure, not
measured tyre slip. A comparable sector must have 85% observed coverage,
no interruption or fee, and at least 0.5 seconds active duration.
The standing-start sector cannot become a rolling reference.

The Results screen shows each gate's most recent row, coverage, sampled
mean and peak speed, path length, conditional durations and same-gate
comparison against a prior qualified rolling reference. The live HUD
can show the last matched comparison. No causal claim is made from
differences in these observations. The ring retains 32 rows while
whole-run per-gate summaries retain bounded counters and references.

Native acceptance: verify normal racing and Time Attack, compare gate
receipts and actual body motion, test pause/recovery and a fee, complete
two laps, verify the first standing-start sector is excluded, restart
and confirm old comparisons disappear. Source tests do not establish
native gameplay acceptance.
