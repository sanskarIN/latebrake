# Sampled speed distribution

The Results page includes average sampled planar speed, time spent in
seven speed bands and the median/P90 speed bands. Quantiles are
time-weighted intervals, not interpolated exact speeds or performance
ratings. Samples are admitted by existing DrivingSessionStats policy;
hitches and invalid velocity do not enter the histogram. Saturation or
unreconciled totals suppress the histogram while retaining prior
basic telemetry behavior. The report does not affect physics, awards
or saved records.

Native acceptance: drive at varied speeds and stop briefly; check the
mean, sample percentages, compact layout, and paginated expanded Results.
Pause/resume, recover, restart, and verify only accepted samples enter the
distribution. No native Unreal compile/render/cooked build is established
by portable tests.
