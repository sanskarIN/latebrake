# Game design — Latebrake

**Tagline:** Race Beyond Limits.

**Vision:** an original, fictional racing universe combining satisfying handling with exploration and motorsport events. A tight, replayable starter circuit comes first; expansive open-world content follows proven performance and stable controls.

## Handling

Accessible Arcade mode provides more acceleration and lateral assistance. Simulation-inspired mode reduces artificial grip and alters steering response. Neither represents a validated vehicle simulation yet. Future Chaos Vehicles tires, powertrain, tuning and suspension must be data-driven and carefully playtested.

## First-play loop

Spawn on the starter loop; follow the four checkpoints in order; complete three laps; compare the finished time with your saved personal best; press T to replay. Players can recover and pause without losing offline access. On the v0.1.1 development branch, each recovery during an active race adds five seconds to discourage skipping course sectors.

## Future modes and systems

v0.2.0 source adds experimental circuit rival AI, one-lap Time Attack and offline finish rewards; actual Unreal gameplay and AI race results remain unverified. Sprint, drifting, championships, traffic, garage and multiplayer are future milestones. Offline racing remains a product requirement independent of online services.

## Accessibility and design standards

Readable race telemetry, separate keyboard/gamepad/touch control layouts, remapping, motion-reduction options, scalable interfaces, difficulty controls and audio settings belong in release acceptance. Avoid pay-to-win mechanics: voluntary support is independent from race results, progression and purchases.

## Art ownership

Use original fictional vehicles, fictional locations, independently authored sound/music, and fully documented asset provenance. Avoid unlicensed manufacturer logos, copied tracks, leaked or restricted engine resources.

## Garage prototype — v0.3.0

Three fictional tunable prototype vehicles (Arrow, Kestrel and Phantom) allow offline choice between acceleration, handling and top speed. Vehicle unlocks and engine/grip upgrades spend credits earned only from completed races. Donations never affect garage progression. The current user interface consists of keyboard shortcuts and HUD status; original 3D vehicle art, a full garage menu and gamepad/mobile interactions remain future work.
